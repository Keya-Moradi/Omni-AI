Welcome to the new space. I wanted a quiet, minimal home for my work that feels closer to an
operating system than a marketing site. Astro keeps the output lean, MDX keeps writing flexible, and
Tailwind lets me dial in the layout without a lot of custom CSS.

## A few principles

- Keep the surface area small and focused.
- Prefer static files and predictable deployments.
- Write in public as a forcing function for clarity.

This site is intentionally simple so I can keep shipping without friction. Expect short updates,
project logs, and the occasional deeper dive on process.
